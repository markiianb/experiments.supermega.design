Not a reproduction. Manfred Mohr has asked that his artwork not be reproduced online, and nothing here reproduces it. Every image on this page is generated by code written from the published description of the mechanism — no scan, no trace, no copy of an original plot. Documented mechanism
Four numbered graphic units from two routines: matrix labyrinths, and interpolation with hatching between curves. Placement is calculated by a magic square whose sum is fifteen.
Interpretation
A five-symbol Latin magic field, exact placement families, geometric constants, and the fifth symbol as configurable negative space.
Supermega
Deterministic seeds, alternate mappings, build playback, contemporary palettes, replayable configuration, and fixed-size PNG export.
