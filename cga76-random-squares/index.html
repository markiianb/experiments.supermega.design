Random Squares

Documented mechanism
Franke distributed square outlines independently in three planes. Each plane had a predefined size grid, count, distribution, and random draw. Plotter versions included red, blue, and black.
Interpretation
Exact counts, margins, concentration curves, and seeded consumption constants are reconstructed. One fixed tape reserves equal draw budgets for all layers.
Supermega
Layer isolation, interleaved plotting, contemporary palette, replayable configuration, plot playback, and fixed PNG export.
