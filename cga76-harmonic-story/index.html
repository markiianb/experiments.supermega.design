Harmonic Story

Documented mechanism
Marquette converted letters into fixed-width binary groups, printed six-, seven-, and eight-bit listings, and translated selected windows into visual works.
Interpretation
The historical codebook, bit order, punctuation rules, and final composition are unknown. This tool names its modern codebooks and uses a 44-line by 36-column source matrix.
Supermega
Seeded crop-layer composition, colored offsets, broad framing bands, inversion, URL replay, and fixed PNG export.
