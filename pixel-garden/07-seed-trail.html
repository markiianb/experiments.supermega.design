seed trail

your cursor sheds pixels as it moves. shed them over the soil at the bottom of the screen and they take root — a seed sits for a second, then a flower comes up.

wiggle the cursor over the soil, then pause. the dropped pixels do the rest.

the garden remembers

every flower you plant is stored in this browser under the key pg-seed-garden. close the tab, come back next week — the bed replants itself, one flower at a time.

the bed holds 120 plants. past that, the oldest flower composts itself to make room for yours.

why we built it

most cursor effects evaporate the moment you stop moving. this one leaves something behind — a garden you grew half by accident, still standing on your next visit.

keep scrolling. the soil stays with you.
