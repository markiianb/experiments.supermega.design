what this is — the work section as a garden. one flower per project: species is the service, height is the engagement length, left to right is ship order. hover a flower for the project, click to open its case study. sample data.

a portfolio that grows

every shipped project plants a flower. product work comes up as sunflowers, web as poppies, brand as tulips, motion as cornflowers, advisory as lavender. the longer the engagement, the taller the plant.

the bed replants itself in ship order on every visit — the newest project is always the last one to come up.
