Supermega

Pixel garden — header effect research

What this is

A recreation study of the config.floguo.com header: a procedural pixel-art scene drawn on canvas with a 4px grid of 3×3 squares, halftone clouds, ASCII-sprite flowers that grow in with an expo ease and sparkle flicker, drifting clouds, and hidden hover easter eggs.

Move the mouse anywhere to see the pixel cursor trail: positions quantized to a 6px grid, cycling color palette, alpha ramp toward the head, and a tail-first collapse after 80ms of idle.

Hover around the sky in the header — a few easter eggs are hiding there.
