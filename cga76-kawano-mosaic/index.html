Kawano Mosaic

Documented mechanism
Kawano analyzed data pictures into transition characteristics, then used a computer to generate new pictures. The issue shows raw grid output and a transformed result.
Interpretation
An order-two model uses left, left-two, above, and above-two contexts with smoothing and deterministic backoff. Exact historical tables are unavailable.
